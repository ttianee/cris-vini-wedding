Deluxe Apartment

Designed for comfort, practicality, and well-being, this option suits couples and families. It has a balcony and accommodation options for up to five guests, including units adapted for guests with disabilities (PCD).

Deluxe Chalet

Set along charming garden pathways, the chalet offers a welcoming and peaceful setting for families. It is a short walk from the restaurants, main pool, and baby kitchen. An adapted unit for guests with disabilities (PCD) is also available.

Villa Premium

A modern, more exclusive option in a convenient resort location. It includes a hammock balcony, air conditioning, and a safe. An adapted unit for guests with disabilities (PCD) is available.

Exclusive Villa Premium benefits

  • Stocked minibar: exclusive products such as water, beer, and soft drinks, replenished during the stay.
  • Room service: available from 10 pm to 5 am.
  • Coffee machine: capsule coffee machine in the room.
  • Lunch on check-out day: lunch included on the day of departure.
  • SPA discount: 20%–25% off massages and treatments at Kalanchoe SPA, depending on the current offer.
  • Technology and connectivity: premium Wi-Fi and Smart TV with streaming apps.
  • Special amenities: O Boticário personal care and toiletry products.
How to book using the wedding rate
  1. Contact the resort using the phone number the couple will share with guests.
  2. Tell the team that your reservation is for the wedding of Cristiane and Vinicius, so they can identify you as a wedding guest.

AIRPORT & TRANSFER

Getting there

The closest airport is Ilhéus – Jorge Amado Airport (IOS).

Distance

Approximately 22–24 km from the resort.

Estimated journey

Around 30 minutes by car or taxi, depending on traffic.

RouteThe route generally follows the BA-001 highway through the Águas de Olivença area.
